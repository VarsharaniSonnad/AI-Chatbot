intro
Overview
Agedcare providers must balance compassionate, personcentred support with stringent funding, compliance, and workforce pressures. Fragmented systems and manual processes often make it hard to see the full picture of a resident’s needs, family expectations, and service history. Alphaleo uses Salesforce Public Sector and Health Cloud to help agedcare organisations create a single, connected view of residents, homecare clients, and carers, linking clinical, operational, and engagement data into one platform. This allows teams to coordinate care, respond faster, and keep families informed, while embedding quality, safety, and regulatory requirements into everyday workflows.

Our Offerings
Resident & Client 360
Unified profiles for residents, homecare clients, and family contacts, including care plans, preferences, interactions, and history.
Care & Service Management
Case and service workflows for assessments, care plans, incident reporting, service delivery, and followup.
Care Worker & Provider Coordination
Rostering, visit management, and task assignment for infacility and inhome care teams, integrated with HR and payroll systems.
Family & Carer Portals

Configure Salesforce Public Sector or Health Cloud to reflect resident journeys from intake and assessment to ongoing care and review.
Connect Care Teams & Systems
Integrate with clinical, rostering, HR, and finance systems ensure relevant data is visible to frontline and backoffice teams.

intro
Overview
As enterprises move beyond rule-based automation and reactive AI, a new paradigm is emerging Agentic AI . Unlike traditional AI systems that respond only to predefined inputs, agentic systems are designed to act with intent. They can reason over context, plan multiple steps ahead, interact with tools and systems, and autonomously execute tasks to achieve defined business objectives.
A lphaleo’s Agentic AI services enable organizations to build intelligent, goal driven agents that operate seamlessly across enterprise platforms, data sources, and applications. By combining advanced reasoning, multi-step planning, tool and API integration, and retrieval augmented generation (RAG), we design agents capable of managing complex workflows ranging from proactive process orchestration to dynamic decision-making across functions.
Our agentic AI solutions are built to augment human intelligence, not replace it. They empower teams with AI copilots and autonomous agents that adapt to changing conditions, learn from context, and continuously optimize outcomes. Designed with enterprise governance, security, and scalability in mind, Alphaleo’s agentic systems help organizations unlock higher levels of productivity, responsiveness, and operational intelligence.

Our Offerings

•	Multi-step reasoning and goal-based planning systems
•	Tool and API-integrated agents for enterprise platforms
•	Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG) enabled agents
•	AI copilots embedded within business applications
•	Governance, safety, and observability frameworks
What We Deliver
•	AI agents that automate complex, end-to-end workflows
•	Faster decision cycles through intelligent reasoning systems
•	Reduced operational overhead and manual intervention
•	Seamless human AI collaboration via copilots and assistants
•	Scalable agent frameworks aligned with enterprise controls
Why Alphaleo

Turning CCTV Into a Workforce Intelligence Platform
Workforce Intelligence Platform
Alphaleo's VisionBenchAI leverages artificial intelligence and computer vision to transform standard CCTV infrastructure into a real‑time workforce intelligence platform. The solution continuously analyses worker movement, safety behaviour, and operational patterns to provide supervisors with actionable insights — without requiring additional hardware investments.
Worker Productivity Monitoring
Continuously monitors worker movement and activity to identify idle or unproductive periods using AI‑based pose and motion analysis. Tracks active working hours, idle duration, and productivity trends across workers, zones, and shifts.
Group Behaviour Analytics
Detects prolonged worker gatherings by identifying clusters of two or more individuals in non‑designated areas and evaluating duration and context. Distinguishes operational collaboration from non‑productive group activity.
PPE Compliance Monitoring
Performs real‑time detection of critical PPE — helmets, safety vests, gloves, goggles, boots, and face masks — for every worker on site. Instantly identifies violations and generates automated alerts with photographic evidence.
Intelligent Alerts & Workforce Insights
Delivers real‑time notifications for safety violations, idle workers, and unauthorized gatherings through configurable alert channels, plus interactive dashboards for productivity and compliance trends.

Giving Back: The Alphaleo Incubation Centre
In 2025, Alphaleo took a decisive step to extend its impact beyond boardrooms and into the classrooms of our founder’s alma mater. The Alphaleo Incubation Centre at P.D.A. College of Engineering, Kalaburagi, is a stateoftheart hub designed to nurture the next generation of tech leaders where our story began. More than an incubation space, it is a launchpad for ideas, careers, and confidence.
The centre’s mission is simple and powerful: hire, train, and enable local talent. Through handson workshops, mentorship, and realworld projects in Salesforce, Databricks, and AI, students transform theoretical learning into industryready skills. In its early cohorts, the program has trained interns and transitioned many into fulltime Alphaleo roles, actively bridging the urban–rural tech divide in the Kalyana Karnataka region.

Alphaleo helps manufacturers use Salesforce anchored in Manufacturing Cloud, to connect demand, production, and service on a single, datadriven platform. Experience
Salesforce Manufacturing Cloud - Our Industry Experience
Factory & Custom Builds
Alphaleo works with OEM and custom build manufacturers that need tighter alignment between the shop floor and customer commitments. Implementations connect Manufacturing Cloud with ERP, MES, and IoT platforms so production planners and account teams see the same truth, what has been sold, what is in production, and where risks exist. Data from distributor pipelines and point of sale feeds back into Salesforce, improving demand forecasting, rebate management, and territory planning. As a result, manufacturers and distributors can reduce stockouts and excess inventory, coordinate promotions more effectively, and serve end customers with greater reliability and speed.

A well-planned Salesforce Sales Cloud implementation can transform these scattered touchpoints into a unified, revenue-generating guest journey.
Key Hospitality Challenges Sales Cloud Can Solve
•	Fragmented guest profiles across booking engines, PMS, loyalty, and OTAs
•	Limited visibility into corporate and group deals
•	Manual sales tasks, follow-ups, and approvals that slow down conversions
•	Inconsistent upsell and cross-sell processes across properties
How Alphaleo Approaches Sales Cloud for Hospitality
At Alphaleo, Sales Cloud implementations start with your guest journey, not just your org structure. We map how prospects discover, evaluate, book, stay, and return, then design Sales Cloud to support that lifecycle end-to-end.
Our typical approach includes:
1. Discovery and Journey Mapping
•	Understand your B2C and B2B revenue streams (corporate accounts, events, groups, loyalty)
•	Identify gaps in lead capture, follow-up, and reporting
•	Prioritize use cases like upsell at booking, group sales, and corporate rate management
2. Data and System Integration
•	Design integrations with PMS, channel managers, booking engines, and loyalty platforms
•	Build a unified guest and account 360° view that sales teams can trust
•	Define data models for properties, room types, corporate contracts, and packages
3. Sales Process Design and Automation
•	Configure lead and opportunity stages aligned to your hospitality sales motions
•	Automate tasks like follow-up reminders, proposal approvals, and contract renewals
•	Enable guided selling for upsell opportunities (room upgrades, add-on experiences, F&B, events)
4. Forecasting and Revenue Visibility
•	Provide real-time visibility into pipeline by market, property, and segment
•	Enable better collaboration between revenue management and sales teams
•	Surface insights on win rates, lead sources, and campaign effectiveness
Use Cases That Deliver Quick Wins

•	Centralize all corporate accounts, RFPs, and group inquiries in Sales Cloud
•	Use standardized quote templates and approval flows for faster turnaround times
Loyalty and Repeat Business
•	Give sales and front-office teams visibility into guest history, preferences, and previous stays

Upsell and Cross-Sell
•	Track and automate upsell opportunities (spa, events, dining experiences)
•	Use Sales Cloud data to support dynamic offer strategies in marketing and front desk operations
Change Management and User Adoption
Technology is only as strong as the teams using it. Alphaleo supports:
•	Role-based training for sales, reservations, revenue, and leadership
•	Best-practice playbooks for pipeline management and account planning
•	Governance and enhancement roadmaps to ensure Sales Cloud evolves with your properties and markets
Why Alphaleo
